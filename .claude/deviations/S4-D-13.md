# S4-D-13 — Lean audit-bound predicate v0: deviations & notes

## Placement

The ticket's FILES list says `biject/PolicyEnv/AuditBound.lean` and
`biject/PolicyEnv/Contract.lean`. The platform's existing Lean package lives in
`bijectai/biject-api` at `lean-worker/PolicyEnv/`, but this sprint's work was
explicitly scoped to **not** modify `biject-api`. So the predicate ships here as
a **standalone Lake project** at `PolicyEnv/` (library layout
`PolicyEnv/PolicyEnv/*.lean`, matching the platform package so the two modules
can be dropped into `lean-worker/PolicyEnv/PolicyEnv/` unchanged — plus two
`import` lines in the root `PolicyEnv.lean` — when the demo predicate is
promoted to the platform kernel pool). Toolchain pinned to the same
`leanprover/lean4:v4.28.0` as the platform package for exactly that reason.

**Consequence:** a `/api/verify` call on the platform will NOT evaluate
`AuditEntryValid` until that promotion happens (same class of gap as the
pharmacy vertical's metadata-only status). The demo wiring ticket that consumes
this (S4-A-11 verify-before-forward) needs either (a) the promotion into
`biject-api`'s PolicyEnv + registry seeding, or (b) a worker pointed at this
repo's compiled `.olean`s. Decide at D2 wiring time; log the choice here.

## Ticket-text fixes carried in (from the v3 review)

- `oldValueHash`/`newValueHash` are **bound**, not dangling: a `modify` with
  equal hashes is refuted, and a `modify` with empty `oldValueHash` is refuted.
- No `ByteVec 32` in the kernel — hashes are opaque `String`s; `sigOk : Bool`
  is the declared trust boundary.
- `nowMs` is proxy-supplied and documented as the second trusted input, in the
  same paragraph as `sigOk` (`Contract.lean`, "Trust boundary").
- Strict monotonicity (`ledgerHeadTsMs < tsUnixMs`) breaks under concurrent
  writers — recorded as a known limitation for the single-agent demo.

## Skew constant

`forwardSkewMs = 300000` (5 min) — covers NTP drift between harness and proxy
on the demo host(s). S4-D-30 (Day 3) re-justifies this against 21 CFR Part 11
§11.10(e) and ties monotonicity to the real Merkle ledger head; v0 takes
`ledgerHeadTsMs` as a context input supplied by the proxy from the chain.
